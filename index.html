Hi, I'm Srishti!!!

I'm eighteen and from Delhi, and this page is about the books and movies I can't stop obsessing over.

Books

The first book I ever read was a Tinkle comic, and I've been hooked on stories ever since. These days I read mostly myth and retellings of stuff, which is why Madeline Miller's Circe and The Song of Achilles sit so high on my list. I also love The Odyssey, Amish's Shiva Trilogy and Ram Chandra series (War of Lanka is my favourite), and ABSOLUTELY ANYTHING by Sudha Murty. Also, I can live without Percy Jackson and Harry Potter.

Movies

On the screen side I'm a superhero person through and through, so Marvel and DC take up a lot of my watchlist. I'm also loyal to the Harry Potter and Percy Jackson worlds, and I like comparing how a story feels in the book with how it looks on screen.

The Avengers

If I had to pick one corner of Marvel to live in, it would be the Avengers films, because they pulled so many separate stories into one big shared world and made it work. THEY ARE MY INSPIRATION, THE LOVE OF MY LIFE AND JUST EVERYTHING FOR ME!!!
